15 of 30. That's how many AI benchmarks Epoch AI has audited and rated Flawed.

The list includes SWE-bench Verified, SWE-Bench Pro, Terminal-Bench 4.0.0 and OSWorld 2.1. Six were Verified. Nine could not be reviewed because the tasks or settings are private.

Why it matters if you build with agents: when you pick a model or harness on a three-point gap, you are trusting the grader as much as the model.

How the audit works:
- Sample about 50 tasks. An AI pass flags errors, a second pass tries to disprove them, and humans check what is left.
- Count two failure types: tasks that pass without the work, and correct work that gets rejected.
- Rate the benchmark Flawed when errors affect at least 20% of the score.

In Agents' Last Exam v1.1, 22 of 50 sampled tasks could earn full marks through a shortcut. In OSWorld 2.1, Epoch counted 24 cases of correct work rejected against 12 of wrong work passed.

One caveat: the Agents' Last Exam team checked its recorded runs and found only 3 of 1,617 took a flagged shortcut. A shortcut that exists is different from one agents take.

Question for you: which number matters more, 22 of 50 or 3 of 1,617?
