Gemini 4 Hydrogen scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis index. Argon scores 50. The interesting number is somewhere else: 24% less quota.

That is what Google says its Antigravity Orchestrator saves while keeping near-Carbon task success on Terminal-Bench 4.0, and finishing 19% faster.

Why it matters to anyone building agents: the gain comes from how models are combined, not from a smarter model.

What I took from Google's post:
- Carbon coordinates the plan
- Hydrogen runs worker and verifier agents in isolated workspaces
- The routing logic and baseline are not disclosed, so treat the numbers as vendor-reported

Early independent checks are mixed: faster on a vision bench, but one test saw Hydrogen cost more because it kept re-verifying its own work.
