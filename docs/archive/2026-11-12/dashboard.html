LEAD STORY Primary (paper + DeepMind post, fetched) + The Register + Qodo. Authors' claims; outside validation limited.

AlphaProof Code: agent teams wrote ~6.5M lines of Lean to verify Signal's compiled code, resting on ~26,000 trusted lines

DeepMind's paper pairs human-approved formal specs with teams of prover agents; Lean's kernel and a gatekeeper accept only proofs of the held-out spec. Scope: one-to-one sessions, crypto primitives as contracts. Four weeks, five people; no dollar cost reported.

Why it matters: The trust sits in the spec and the checker, not the agents. A pattern to copy for any unattended agent work.

Primary (Goodfire page, fetched; first 100k of 163k characters read) + TLDR AI (inbox). Single-model study.

Goodfire: individual LLM neurons may be more interpretable than assumed, in a Qwen3.6-35B-A3B study

Keeping the top 320 of 4,096 routed neurons per token (92.2% of the rest ablated) raised web-text loss from 1.886 to 1.940. About 95% detection of strong activations against random text. Results come from one model family and partly rely on LLM judges.

Why it matters: Another route to interpretability besides sparse autoencoders; read the caveats before generalizing.

Primary (repo README, fetched) + TLDR AI (inbox). Authors' claims, not independently verified.

Meta FAIR's e-means: drop-in k-means replacement claiming ~10x fewer epochs and lower final loss

Part of faiss contrib; uses exponential-moving-average centroid updates with no extra hyperparameters. The README notes GPU input is not checked for NaN, FP16 overflows silently above 65,520, and the fast kernels need H100/H200 or A100-class GPUs.

Why it matters: Worth a benchmark on your clustering workloads; the speedups are the authors' own.

Antigravity /swarm previews coordinator, worker and verifier agents on a shared message board

A coordinator splits work into named workers with clean contexts; a verifier reviews diffs and runs tests; workers talk only through one ordered board. Google warns swarms burn quota much faster than a single conversation. Available on AI Pro and Ultra in preview.

Primary (PyTorch blog, fetched) + TLDR AI (inbox). Project's own benchmarks.

ExecuTorch WebGPU runs PyTorch models in the browser GPU: 4-7x faster prefill and 2-10x faster decode than its wasm CPU path

Export once with torch.export to a .pte file and run it in any WebGPU browser. Benchmarks use quantized Llama 3.2 1B and Qwen3-0.6B in Chrome on an M1 Pro. The backend is early-stage.

Why it matters: A path to on-device inference without converting to ONNX or GGUF; check operator coverage for your model first.

Primary (repo README, fetched) + TLDR Dev (inbox). Author's benchmark.

chrome-capture: Chrome's native screen recording cuts stop-and-finalize from 3.8 s to 17 ms versus Playwright recordVideo

Uses the Page.startScreenRecording CDP method to encode AV1 video inside Chrome instead of piping JPEG frames to ffmpeg. The README benchmark is one 6.4-second replay on an M4 Max; at 2160p stop time is 91 ms versus 20.5 s.

Why it matters: Faster, lighter browser-test recordings for agent debugging; single-machine benchmark, so measure on your CI.
