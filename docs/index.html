Research

★ LEAD Primary x2 (paper, repo README) + AlphaSignal newsletter. Speedups are authors' own.

Latent Sparse Attention: 22.7x faster prefill at 10M tokens by clustering keys instead of scanning them

Paper (arXiv 2610.42147, Oct 30, v2 Nov 3) says that at 10M tokens the key-selection step dominates sparse attention. LSA clusters keys in a learned space so queries score centroids. Reported: 22.7x prefill / 4.6x decode over GLM-5.3-Flash's sparse attention for one layer; 2.2x end-to-end first-token gain on GLM and 9.2x on Qwen vs full attention. Gains shrink at shorter context.

Why it matters: Long-running agents make selection cost, not attention, the latency lever; code and weights are Apache-2.0.
