Trial by Trace: a benchmark grades agent harnesses separately from task success, and 31.1% of successful runs still failed at least one harness criterion

Submitted to arXiv Oct 23, 2026. Four harnesses (Claude Code, Codex, DeepSeek Harness, OpenClaw) and nine model groups across 95 tasks and 3,420 runs. Harness macro scores range from 59.8% to 72.0%, and the best task-pass rate (Claude Code, 44.0%) is not the best reliability score. Only 8.4% of runs pass every criterion in both layers. The judge is a fixed model with no reported agreement figures.

Why it matters: Outcome-only evals can count broken harness runs as wins. Log tool calls, grade them, and treat the harness as a system in its own right.

AlphaProof Code: DeepMind agents wrote about six million lines of Lean to verify key security properties of Signal's code

Covered as the Nov 12 lead. Human experts approve the specifications, agents flag about 1% of the codebase for human review, and Lean checks the proofs. Verification took four weeks with a head start from earlier Signal work. Two reintroduced bugs were both flagged as insecure by the proofs.

Why it matters: The trust sits in the specification and the checker, not in the agents. Line counts differ across reports (six million vs 6.5 million).
